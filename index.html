About

Planet Sound

An open map of the world's sounds — contributed by anyone, curated by us.

Every entry sits at a place and a moment. The archive begins now and grows forward: nothing is backfilled, nothing is invented. What you hear here is what someone stood in and chose to keep.

LocationWhere a sound happened
TimeWhen it happened, and what it meant
OpennessNo expertise required to take part
CompassionEasy to give a sound, easy to receive one

Adding a sound never requires an account. When you record, you decide whether it joins the public map or stays in a private archive only you can see — and you can move it between the two at any time, or take it down entirely. Nobody else can alter or remove what you recorded.

An account is worth making if you want a private archive, or if you'd like to reach your recordings from more than one device.
